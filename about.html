About

Hi, I'm Osamah.

I'm a network and systems administrator in Dearborn, Michigan. I've spent nearly seven years keeping networks, servers and services reliable for organizations that can't afford downtime and usually can't afford much else either.

My work covers the full stack of a small organization: the firewall and switches, the domain controllers, Microsoft 365, the phones, the cameras, and the backups behind all of it. I like the jobs where I get to design something properly from the start, and I'm comfortable with the jobs where I inherit a mess and have to stabilize it first.

How I work

I studied philosophy, and it shows in how I approach IT. I care about whether a design holds together: whether the permissions model makes sense, whether the backup plan works when tested, and whether the next person can understand it from the documentation. I would rather fix the cause than patch the symptom and leave a mess for later.

The same habit makes me good at explaining technical trade-offs to people who aren't technical, such as business owners, nonprofit boards and volunteers.

Where I'm headed

I'm finishing my CCNA (expected November 2026) and moving on to Microsoft AZ-802 (expected January 2027). Longer term, I'm working toward systems engineering and solutions architecture: designing infrastructure across on-premises and cloud for organizations bigger than the ones I support today.
